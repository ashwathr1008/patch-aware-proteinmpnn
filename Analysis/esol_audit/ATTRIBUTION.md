# Attribution for redistributed data

`esol_mapped.csv` contains solubility values derived from the eSOL database, together with
identifiers and PDB cross-references from UniProt. Redistributing it carries obligations.

**eSOL.** Required attribution, reproduced verbatim as the licence specifies:

> eSOL © Hideki Taguchi (Tokyo Institute of Technology) licensed under CC Attribution-Share
> Alike 2.1 Japan

Source: <https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/data/esol/>. Primary reference: Niwa T., Ying B.-W.,
Saito K., Jin W., Takada S., Ueda T., Taguchi H. "Bimodal protein solubility distribution
revealed by an aggregation analysis of the entire ensemble of *Escherichia coli* proteins."
*PNAS* 106(11):4201–4206, 2009.

**Share-alike applies.** CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan requires derivative works to be distributed under
the same or a compatible licence. This file and `esol_mapped.csv` are a derivative of eSOL.
Before the final report is published, the team should decide whether to keep the per-record
table in the repository under a compatible licence, or to retain only the aggregate statistics
in `esol_audit.json`, which are facts about the dataset rather than a redistribution of it.

**UniProt.** Accessions, gene names, ordered locus names and PDB cross-references are from
UniProt, released under CC BY 4.0. Source: <https://www.uniprot.org/>.
